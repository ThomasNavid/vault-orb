# Life Admin

Keep your own career documents and certificates in the folders below. No records are included. Link a document from the Life task that needs it instead of copying it.

- `6. Life Admin/Career/CVs`
- `6. Life Admin/Career/Certificates`

Financial records, imports, reports, and dashboards are not part of this starter. Store sensitive documents only where you intend them to be available to your tools and backups.
